# Product

## Register

product

## Users

General players taking part in a short Mario-style mini-game on desktop or the project's existing touch controls. They need to understand the objective, remaining lives, time pressure, coin progress, and final result without learning a complex ruleset.

## Product Purpose

Provide one fair, replayable platforming challenge that can be clearly won or lost and awards a transparent score from 0 to 200. A perfect run reaches the flag quickly, collects every coin, and loses no lives. The built-in mini-game is the scored experience; editor-created levels remain compatible with their legacy behavior.

## Brand Personality

Playful, energetic, and legible. The experience should feel recognizably inspired by the project's existing classic platform-game presentation while making competitive rules and feedback immediately clear.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the game into a modern dashboard, cover the playfield with panels, introduce unrelated visual styles, or reward obscure and farmable actions. Avoid unreadable overlays, unexplained score changes, unavoidable damage, blind jumps, and difficulty based on unreliable precision.

## Design Principles

- Make win, loss, and the 200-point path obvious.
- Let challenge rise through readable platforming patterns.
- Keep competitive scoring deterministic and resistant to farming.
- Preserve the existing game's character and control model.
- Prefer immediate feedback and a concise result breakdown.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use text as well as color for time and outcome states, maintain strong contrast over the sky background, preserve keyboard and touch controls, expose result controls as real buttons, and respect clear focus states. Avoid relying on animation alone to communicate a state change.
